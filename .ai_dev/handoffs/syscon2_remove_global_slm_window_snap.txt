syscon2_remove_global_slm_window_snap — Remove obsolete "snap to allowed windows" sanity step
==============================================================================================

The SysCon2 sanity-check flow used to detect stim starts that didn't align to the
global 16.666 ms SLM grid and prompt the user with three buttons:
"Snap To Allowed Windows" / "Continue Without Fix" / "Cancel". With the
spiralling + preblank-insertion pipeline in place, the exporter's internal
alignment handles this automatically — the upfront snap-or-warn dance is dead
weight that confuses users. Remove the entire detection-and-fix step (step 5 of
`_run_sanity_check`) along with the now-unused exporter helpers and their tests.

Files to change:

    src/neuroseq/exporter_syscon2.py
    src/neuroseq/stimulation_generator_dialog.py
    tests/test_stimulation_generator_dialog.py

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RATIONALE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The exporter's preblank-window insertion in `_apply_preblank_insertions_to_raw_segments`
already shifts every raw stim segment forward by one full 16.666 ms SLM window,
guaranteeing that the resulting `adjusted_raw_segment.start_idx` is always on
the global grid (modulo sub-window resolution, which is fine for TTL pulses but
irrelevant for shape windows). Asking the user to manually snap their raw
drawing to the same grid before that automatic shift happens is redundant —
the snap-fix never had a different output than letting the pipeline proceed
without it.
